Payment options

Pay whichever way is easiest for you. Every gift is optional, and every gift is confirmed against our payment records before it shows on the progress bar.

MTN Mobile Money

Send to 0789291098 (Rogers Kitamirike).

If the button does not open, dial *165# and follow the steps below.

Follow these steps
  1. Dial *165#.
  2. Select option 1 (Send Money).
  3. Select Mobile User.
  4. Enter number 0789291098 (Rogers Kitamirike).
  5. Enter the amount you wish to give.
  6. Enter your PIN.

The sending fee is taken from your MTN balance automatically, so we receive the full amount you type. It is 500 UGX for 5,001 to 60,000 and 1,000 UGX for 60,001 to 500,000. The smallest transfer MTN accepts is 500 UGX.

MTN Uganda published Mobile Money Tariffs, mtn.co.ug/tariffs/mobile-money-tariffs

World Remit

Send money online from anywhere in the world to Uganda, to our mobile money numbers or to our bank account above.

If the button does not open, go to worldremit.com, choose Uganda and send to the number or account above.

World Remit charges its own fee, and the transfer takes a few days to arrive. We confirm it against their record once it reaches us.

PayPal

Pay with a card, a PayPal balance, or PayPal. Choose any amount you like.

If the button does not open, go to paypal.me/newlugandahymnal and pay any amount you choose.

PayPal charges its own fee, which for a card payment can come out of the amount you send. We confirm the gift against the PayPal record.
